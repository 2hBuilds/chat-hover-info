## What it shows

- **Drops.** A clan or friends broadcast of a drop, or your own: the item's price, its alch value, where it drops and how rarely, and the wiki's first paragraph about it.
- **Quests, combat tasks, diaries.** The quest's difficulty, length and points; the task's tier, points, boss and what it asks; the diary's tier.
- **Pets.** Where the pet comes from, its drop rate, and the kill count it dropped at.
- **Levels.** What a new level unlocks, from the wiki's level-up tables. Total levels and XP milestones too.
- **Kill counts and personal bests.** The game's own, and the answers to a clan member's `!kc` and `!pb`, with the boss's page a click away.

## Quick guide

1. **Hover.** Rest the mouse on the item, quest, task or pet name in a broadcast. Names you can hover are blue; level lines underline when the mouse is on them.
2. **Open the wiki.** Right-click the line and pick "Open wiki", or "Copy" to copy the name.
3. **Place the card.** Above the cursor, or as a wide card pinned above the chatbox: "Card place" in the settings.
4. **Show it your way.** On hover, while a key is held, or on click, with a delay if you like, and your own text size.
5. **Quiet it down.** Turn the blue words off, or the wiki paragraphs, in the settings menu: the settings icon on the sidebar panel.

## Good to know

- **It never reads what players type.** Only the game's broadcasts, your own drop lines, and RuneLite's own kill count and personal best answers.
- **The one thing it asks for.** With "Wiki descriptions" on, the name of a card's wiki page is sent to the Old School RuneScape Wiki for that page's first paragraph. Nothing about you, ever. Turn the switch off and nothing is sent.
- **The data.** Quests, tasks, pets, diaries, level unlocks and drop rates are bundled from the <a href="https://oldschool.runescape.wiki">Old School RuneScape Wiki</a> (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0). Prices come from RuneLite.
- **Something wrong?** Tell us in the 2hBuilds Discord - the link is in the settings menu.
